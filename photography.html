  1. Copy full-size images into assets/photos/originals/, with one sub-folder per collection: night-sky, travel, conferences.
  2. Run python3 tools/photos.py. It resizes each image, removes GPS and other metadata from the published copy, and lists it in assets/js/photos.js with its camera settings.
  3. Add titles, places and descriptions in assets/js/photos.js, then commit.
