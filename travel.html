Travel

Travel

Districts of India and cities abroad visited for research, conferences and otherwise. Visited Indian districts are shaded on the India map; cities outside India are pinned on the world map. Drag to pan; zoom with the buttons, a pinch, or Ctrl + scroll; point at a district or pin for details.

Edit mode

Click a district on the India map to mark or unmark it, or find it by name below. On the world map, click a location to add a city pin, or click a pin to change or delete it. Changes stay in this browser. To publish them, copy the data and replace the travel block in assets/js/data.js.
