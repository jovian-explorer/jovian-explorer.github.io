Software

Research code for radio science, planetary data, the solar corona and exoplanet searches. VEDA (version 0.2.0), HELIOS-L1 and COSMIC-2 Explorer (version 0.1.0) are public, occulta is a pre-release (version 0.0.1), and corona3d (version 0.0.1) is still private. Feedback on all five is welcome. Released code is on GitHub.

Scientific software

VEDA

Public · v0.2.0

Desktop laboratory for planetary atmosphere and ionosphere data: finds the products of NASA, ESA, JAXA and ISRO missions for a planet or moon and a date range, plots them, derives physical parameters, compares observations and computes the observation geometry with SPICE.

Planetary atmospheres, multi-mission

COSMIC-2 Explorer

Public · v0.1.0

Desktop app for the COSMIC-2 radio occultation profiles published by UCAR CDAAC: finds, downloads, plots and compares them, computes tropopause, boundary-layer and ionosphere diagnostics, and compares them with weather models, radiosondes and other missions. Works offline once the data are downloaded.

occulta

Pre-release · v0.0.1

Python package for a blind search of TESS, K2 and Kepler photometry for transiting planets and eclipsing binaries that no catalogue lists. It searches every light curve, vets each candidate as a planet and then as an eclipsing binary, assigns the eclipses to their star with difference images of the TESS pixels, and compares eclipse timing with Gaia orbits. The user chooses planet hunting, binary hunting, or both.

Scope
Exoplanets, eclipsing binaries, TESS, K2, Kepler
Platforms
Windows, macOS, Linux

corona3d

Private · v0.0.1

Python package that builds a three-dimensional scene of a coronal mass ejection and the corona around it from public data: the density surface of a PSI MHD model at a radio plasma level coloured by the Alfvén speed, open, closed and streamer field lines, the GCS shell of the CME, radio sources at their Newkirk heights, and LASCO C2 and STEREO-B COR1 and COR2 images in the plane of the sky of their spacecraft. Every layer can be switched on or off.

Scope
Solar corona, CMEs, radio bursts
Platforms
Windows, macOS, Linux
