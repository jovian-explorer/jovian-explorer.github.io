Privacy Policy

Library, a reader for books and research papers. Effective 24 November 2026. About the app

Library is a reading app. It has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no servers that collect your data. Your books, highlights, notes, drawings, recordings and reading statistics are stored on your device. This policy explains the few cases in which the app connects to other services, and only when you ask it to.

What stays on your device

  • Your library, reading positions, highlights, quotes, bookmarks, drawings, notes, voice recordings and reading statistics.
  • Files you import or share to the app, and files you download through it.
  • Your settings, including the folders you choose for your books.
  • Notes the app finds on your device (Google Takeout and Evernote exports, Samsung Notes, Obsidian and Notion folders) and imports into the app.

Android's cloud backup is switched off for the app, so this data is not copied to backups by the system.

Services the app connects to, and when

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
Your Google Drive (app folder)Only if you sign in with Google for syncA private sync file with your library data, stored in a hidden folder of your own Drive that only this app can read.
Google Docs and Google SheetsOnly if you sign in with GoogleLists the names of your documents and spreadsheets, reads and edits the ones you open or create in the app, and keeps the reading status in your spreadsheet named “books” (if you have one) the same as in the app and in Achriom.

Catalogue and download sites you addWhen you browse or downloadOrdinary web requests to the address you chose.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Requests from the app carry no device identifier, no advertising ID and no cookies. Known tracking and analytics servers are blocked by the app.

Google user data

When you connect a Google account, the app uses Google's data only to provide the features you turned on: syncing your library to your own Drive app folder, listing your documents and spreadsheets by name, reading and editing the Google Docs and Sheets you work with in the app, and keeping the reading status in your “books” spreadsheet up to date. Google data is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train any model. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Files and folders: to find and open the books in the folders you choose.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice note; recordings stay on your device.
  • Notifications: for the daily reading reminder and read-aloud controls.
  • Internet: for the services listed above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. Signing out of Google in the app stops sync; you can remove the sync file and the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Data you sent to Achriom is managed in your Achriom account.
