Library

An Android reader for books and research papers, made by Keshav Aggarwal for his own tablet and phone.

The developer of Library receives none of your data. The app has no servers and no accounts of its own, and it never sends anything to the developer: not your books, highlights, notes, reading history, Google files, Achriom library, or any information about you or your device. The developer cannot see any of it. Everything stays on your device, or goes directly from your device to your own Google or Achriom account, and only after you sign in to them.

What it does

  • Opens EPUB, PDF and text books stored on the device, with highlights, quotes, bookmarks, handwritten notes and read-aloud.
  • Shows a bookshelf of everything read, being read and waiting to be read, filtered by author, genre, year and language.
  • Keeps the library the same on every device through a private folder in the user's own Google Drive.
  • Reads and edits the user's Google Docs and Sheets, and keeps a reading-status spreadsheet up to date.
  • Keeps an Achriom reading list up to date when the user signs in to Achriom.
  • Finds the metadata of research papers and imports highlights from annotated PDFs.

Privacy

No data is collected by the developer. The app connects to Google and Achriom only after you sign in, and only to carry your own data between your device and your own accounts. The full policy is on the privacy policy page.
